Privacy Policy

NextWave Estimator is an internal business tool built and used exclusively by NextWave Technologies for its own estimating and invoicing workflow. It is not distributed to, or used by, the general public.

What data this app accesses

This app connects to a single QuickBooks Online company (NextWave Technologies' own account) via the QuickBooks API, to read estimate data (customer names, addresses, and line items) for the purpose of generating invoices. It does not access, store, or share data belonging to any other QuickBooks company or user.

Where data is stored

All data pulled from QuickBooks, along with any manually entered project cost information, is stored on infrastructure owned and operated by NextWave Technologies. No data is shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party beyond QuickBooks itself (as the source of the data) and the infrastructure provider hosting this application.

Data retention

Project and cost data is retained for as long as needed for NextWave Technologies' own business recordkeeping, and can be deleted at any time by the account owner.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to NextWave Technologies directly.
